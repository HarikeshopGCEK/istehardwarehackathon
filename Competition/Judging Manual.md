## EVALUATION CRITERIA

### 1. INNOVATION & CREATIVITY (25 Marks)

**What to Look For:**

- Unique approach to solving the chosen problem
- Creative use of available sensors and components
- Novel features or functionalities
- Original thinking beyond basic requirements

**Evaluation Questions:**

- How original is the solution compared to existing alternatives?
- Does the team demonstrate creative problem-solving?
- Are there innovative features that add value?

**Scoring Guidelines:**

- **Excellent (23-25):** Highly innovative with creative features
- **Good (18-22):** Some creative elements with solid approach
- **Average (13-17):** Standard solution with minimal creativity
- **Below Average (8-12):** Basic implementation, lacks innovation
- **Poor (0-7):** No evidence of creative thinking

**Score:** _____ / 25

---

### 2. FUNCTIONALITY (25 Marks)

**What to Look For:**

- Working prototype that demonstrates core features
- Reliability and consistency of operation
- Completeness of implementation
- Error handling and robustness

**Evaluation Questions:**

- Does the prototype work as intended?
- How reliable is the system during demonstration?
- Are all claimed features actually functional?

**Scoring Guidelines:**

- **Excellent (23-25):** Fully functional with all features working reliably
- **Good (18-22):** Most features work with minor issues
- **Average (13-17):** Core functionality works, some features incomplete
- **Below Average (8-12):** Basic functionality, significant issues
- **Poor (0-7):** Non-functional or major failures

**Score:** _____ / 25

---

### 3. PRACTICAL IMPACT (20 Marks)

**What to Look For:**

- Real-world applicability and usefulness
- Addresses genuine needs or problems
- Scalability and market potential
- User experience and usability

**Evaluation Questions:**

- Would this solution be useful in real-world scenarios?
- Does it address the problem statement effectively?
- Is it user-friendly and accessible?

**Scoring Guidelines:**

- **Excellent (18-20):** High real-world impact and usability
- **Good (14-17):** Good practical value with some limitations
- **Average (10-13):** Moderate practical impact
- **Below Average (6-9):** Limited practical application
- **Poor (0-5):** Little to no real-world value

**Score:** _____ / 20

---

### 4. BUDGET & CONSTRAINT ADHERENCE (15 Marks)

**What to Look For:**

- Compliance with 5-sensor limit
- Staying within Rs. 1500 budget
- Efficient use of resources
- Component selection and optimization

**Pre-calculated Penalties:**

- Budget Penalty: _____ points (5 points per Rs. 25 over limit)
- Sensor Penalty: _____ points (10 points per sensor over limit)

**Evaluation Questions:**

- How well did the team optimize their component selection?
- Did they make smart trade-offs within constraints?

**Scoring Guidelines:**

- **Excellent (14-15):** Optimal resource utilization within all constraints
- **Good (11-13):** Good resource management with minor constraint violations
- **Average (8-10):** Adequate resource use, some inefficiencies
- **Below Average (5-7):** Poor resource optimization
- **Poor (0-4):** Significant constraint violations

**Score before penalties:** _____ / 15  
**Final Score after penalties:** _____ / 15

---

### 5. PRESENTATION & DEMO (15 Marks)

**What to Look For:**

- Clear explanation of problem and solution
- Effective demonstration of functionality
- Professional presentation delivery
- Ability to answer technical questions

**Evaluation Questions:**

- Can the team clearly explain their solution?
- Is the demonstration well-organized and convincing?
- Do they understand their own implementation?

**Scoring Guidelines:**

- **Excellent (14-15):** Outstanding presentation with clear demo
- **Good (11-13):** Good presentation skills with effective demo
- **Average (8-10):** Adequate presentation, demo shows basic functionality
- **Below Average (5-7):** Poor presentation skills or unclear demo
- **Poor (0-4):** Very poor presentation or failed demo

**Score:** _____ / 15
